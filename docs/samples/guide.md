# Field Guide to Tree Frogs

Tree frogs live in forests, gardens and wetlands on every continent except Antarctica.

## Habitat

Most species stay close to water during the breeding season.

### Rainforests

Warm, humid canopies hold the greatest variety.

### Temperate woods

Some species survive winter frozen solid.

## Identification

| Species         | Length | Colour          |
|-----------------|--------|-----------------|
| Green tree frog | 5 cm   | bright green    |
| Grey tree frog  | 4 cm   | grey to green   |

## Calls

Males call at dusk; each species has its own rhythm.
